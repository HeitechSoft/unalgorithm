# Getting help

- **Something hidden that shouldn't be (or not hidden that should)?** Click *Why?* on the post, then *Wrong call?*,
  or use **Report a problem** in the popup. Both open a GitHub issue prefilled with anonymized diagnostics.
- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/HeitechSoft/unalgorithm/discussions).
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/HeitechSoft/unalgorithm/issues/new/choose).
- **Private AI for your organization:** HeitechSoft offers private AI deployments and custom feed policies. Contact
  **hello@heitechsoft.com**.
