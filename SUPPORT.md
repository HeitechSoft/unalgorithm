# Getting help

- **Something hidden that shouldn't be (or not hidden that should)?** Click *Why?* on the post, then *Wrong call?*.
  For anything else that looks wrong on LinkedIn or Facebook, use **Report a problem** in the toolbar popup, or *Let
  us know* when unAlgorithm says something stopped working. Each opens a page in unAlgorithm that shows exactly what
  the report holds (versions, counts and your answers; never post text or names) and sends it to us only when you
  click **Send report**. You don't need an account. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#report-a-problem).
- **A fact-check or UnEcho Chamber result looks wrong?** Choose *Report a wrong verdict* or *Report a mistake* under
  the result. An outlet's lean on unAlgorithm Cloud's source list? See
  [Corrections and appeals](docs/site/corrections.md).
- **Where is Highlights?** Choose **Open Highlights** in the toolbar popup. Chrome and Edge show it in the side
  panel; Firefox shows it in the sidebar, which Alt+Shift+U also opens (Control+Shift+U on a Mac).
- **Anything else, or rather write to us?** Email **support@heitechsoft.com**. If you sent a report, mention its
  reference (such as 7KQ3-M2PA).
- **Private AI for your organization:** HeitechSoft offers private AI deployments and custom feed policies. Contact
  **hello@heitechsoft.com**.
- **Contributing code, fixtures or translations?** GitHub is for contributors:
  [Discussions](https://github.com/HeitechSoft/unalgorithm/discussions) for questions and ideas, and issues for work
  on the code ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Please don't use them to report a problem with your own feed: a
  GitHub issue is public.
