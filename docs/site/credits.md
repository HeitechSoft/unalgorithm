# How credits work

UnAlgorithm Feed is free. Credit pays only for AI that runs on
[UnAlgorithm Cloud](https://cloud.unalgorithm.app), HeitechSoft's hosted AI service at `cloud.unalgorithm.app`.
Everything else in UnAlgorithm Feed costs nothing.

## Starter credit

New verified accounts get {{starterAmount}} of starter credit, once per person. It expires after {{starterDays}} days.
To stop abuse, we may withhold it: one per mailbox, no disposable email addresses, and a daily limit on new grants.
Promotional credit has no cash value.

## When the balance is empty

Cloud AI pauses. Your filters and the results already saved keep working. Add credit, or switch to on-device or your
own key.

## Always free

- Rules and keyword filters.
- On-device AI, where your browser offers it.
- Your own key from an AI provider: you pay the provider directly, not us.
- Self-hosted servers: run the open-source UnAlgorithm server yourself.

There are no subscriptions. Your balance shows in the extension's popup and in Settings. When it runs low, a reminder
shows at most once a day, and never in your feed.
