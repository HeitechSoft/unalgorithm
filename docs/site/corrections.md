# Corrections and appeals

If a Fact-check or UnEcho Chamber result got something wrong, or an outlet's listing or lean on UnAlgorithm Cloud's
source list looks wrong, tell us. We fix causes, publish what changed and count every request in the transparency
reports.

## What never to send

Use your own words: never the post, a screenshot of it, a link to it, or anyone's name. Describe the claim or the story
as you understood it, so your request carries only what you write.

A GitHub username is public on an issue. To ask privately, email the address given below for your request.

## Ask for a correction

Include:

- the feature: Fact-check or UnEcho Chamber;
- the claim or the story, in your own words;
- what it showed (a verdict such as Disputed, or a point of a comparison);
- why it's wrong;
- public sources that show it.

Use the [correction form](https://github.com/HeitechSoft/unalgorithm/issues/new?template=correction.yml), or email
hello@heitechsoft.com.

You get an answer within 5 business days and a decision within 30 days. UnAlgorithm keeps no one's results (they live
only in your browser), so a correction fixes the cause: a prompt, the blocklist, a bug or a model choice. When the
mistake can be reproduced, a made-up example of it joins the evaluation set, so every later change is tested against it.
The outcome is posted on the issue (or answered by email), in the release notes when code changes, and counted in the
next transparency report.

The outcome is one of:

- **Fixed**, with what changed;
- **No change**, with the reason;
- **Out of scope**: an opinion, a prediction, or a claim about a private person.

If the result used your own source list, its leans are yours: change them in Settings → AI.

## Source list appeals

Anyone may appeal, the outlet included, an outlet's lean, name, web addresses or country on UnAlgorithm Cloud's source
list.

Include the outlet's id, as the [source list page](https://cloud.unalgorithm.app/outlets) shows it; what is wrong;
public evidence; and whether you represent the outlet.

Use the [appeal form](https://github.com/HeitechSoft/unalgorithm/issues/new?template=source_list_appeal.yml), or email
legal@heitechsoft.com.

An UnAlgorithm Cloud admin decides within 30 days. When the Cloud has more than one admin, one who did not make the
change appealed decides; while it has one, that admin decides, and the public change history and the quarterly audit
below are the check.

During an appeal the lean stays, unless the admin finds it clearly wrong. Then it is removed at once, and that removal
is published and listed like any change.

The outcome is a change to the list, or none. It is published at once and listed in the change history, the reasons are
given on the issue or by email, and the appeal is counted in the transparency report.

Own lists belong to the people who made them and are not appealed.

## Quarterly audit

Every quarter, an UnAlgorithm Cloud admin reviews:

- every change made to the source list that quarter, from its public change history;
- every outlet appealed;
- a random 10 % of the outlets with a lean, at least 10 (all of them when there are fewer);
- every lean unchanged for more than 12 months;
- every correction;
- the latest live evaluation results.

When the Cloud has more than one admin, changes are reviewed by an admin who did not make them. The findings are
published in that quarter's transparency report.
