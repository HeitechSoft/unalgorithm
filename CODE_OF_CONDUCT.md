# Code of Conduct

UnAlgorithm adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, or trolling.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's personal information, including in fixtures, screenshots or bug reports.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, Discussions, chat) and when you represent the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **conduct@heitechsoft.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the
maintainers, who will respond within a reasonable time and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban
participants, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
