The beta is free. Each account has daily limits on how much it can use. When you reach a limit, the Cloud stops

We may end the beta at any time. Accounts and their data may be reset when it ends or during it. We will tell you

## Acceptable use

- Use the Cloud only through the UnAlgorithm extension and your account page, for your own feeds.
- No automated or bulk use of the API.
- No attempts to extract the prompts, to reach other people's accounts, or to get around the limits.
- No sharing your account or sign-in codes.

If you break these rules, we may remove your access.

The Cloud uses AI to decide which posts to show, and to check claims. AI results can be wrong: posts you wanted can be
hidden, and a fact-check can miss or misjudge a claim. Use your own judgment.
